Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 1-noyabr

Ushbu siyosat «Mudarris Akademiyasi» mobil ilovasi (keyingi o'rinlarda — Ilova) qanday ma'lumotlardan foydalanishini tushuntiradi. Ilova Mudarris Akademiyasi o'quv markazi o'quvchilari va ularning ota-onalari uchun mo'ljallangan.

1. Qanday ma'lumot ishlatiladi

  • O'quvchi ID — kirish uchun kiritiladi (masalan, KX48213). ID o'quv markazi tomonidan beriladi; Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish yo'q.
  • O'quvchi ma'lumotlari — ism-familiya, guruh, baholar, davomat, dars jadvali, to'lovlar, ustoz izohlari. Bular markaz bazasidan faqat ko'rsatish uchun olinadi.
  • O'quv natijalari — Darslar, Monitoring test va Quiz test javoblari hamda natijalari markaz bazasiga yoziladi, ustozlar o'quvchi o'zlashtirishini kuzatishi uchun.
  • Takliflar va murojaatlar — o'zingiz yozib yuborgan matn markazga yetkaziladi.

2. Nima to'planmaydi

  • Joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, fayllar — Ilova bu ruxsatlarni so'ramaydi.
  • Reklama identifikatori, reklama va uchinchi tomon kuzatuv (analytics) vositalari yo'q.
  • Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va reklama maqsadida hech kimga berilmaydi.

3. Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi

Ma'lumotlar o'quv markazining Supabase serverida saqlanadi va faqat shifrlangan (HTTPS) aloqa orqali uzatiladi. Telefonning o'zida faqat kirish holati (o'quvchi ID), mavzu sozlamalari va tezroq ochilish uchun vaqtinchalik nusxa saqlanadi.

4. Kim ko'radi

O'quvchi ma'lumotlarini faqat shu o'quvchi ID si bilan kirgan foydalanuvchi hamda o'quv markazining vakolatli xodimlari ko'radi.

5. Ma'lumotni o'chirish

«Profil → Chiqish» tugmasi telefondagi barcha saqlangan ma'lumotni o'chiradi. Ilovani o'chirib tashlash ham shunday qiladi. Markaz bazasidagi ma'lumotlarni o'chirish yoki tuzatish uchun quyidagi aloqa orqali murojaat qiling — so'rov 30 kun ichida ko'rib chiqiladi.

6. Bolalar

Ilovadan o'quvchilar ota-ona yoki vasiy nazoratida foydalanadi. O'quvchi ID faqat markazda shartnoma tuzilgandan keyin beriladi. Ilova bolalardan reklama yoki marketing uchun ma'lumot yig'maydi.

7. O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, yangi matn shu sahifada sana bilan e'lon qilinadi.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the “Mudarris Akademiyasi” mobile app (hereinafter — the App) uses. The App is intended for students of the Mudarris Akademiyasi learning center and their parents.

1. What information is used

  • Student ID — entered to sign in (for example, KX48213). The ID is issued by the learning center; there is no registration in the App.
  • Student information — full name, group, grades, attendance, class schedule, payments, teacher comments. This is retrieved from the center's database for display only.
  • Learning results — answers and results of Lessons, Monitoring tests and Quiz tests are written to the center's database so that teachers can track the student's progress.
  • Suggestions and requests — text that you write and send yourself is delivered to the center.

2. What is not collected

  • Location, contacts, camera, microphone, files — the App does not request these permissions.
  • No advertising identifier, no ads, and no third-party tracking (analytics) tools.
  • Data is not sold and is not shared with anyone for advertising purposes.

3. Where data is stored

Data is stored on the learning center's Supabase server and is transmitted only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. On the phone itself, only the sign-in state (student ID), theme settings, and a temporary copy for faster loading are stored.

4. Who can see it

A student's information can be seen only by the user who signed in with that student ID and by authorized staff of the learning center.

5. Data deletion

The “Profile → Log out” button deletes all data stored on the phone. Uninstalling the App does the same. To delete or correct data in the center's database, contact us using the details below — requests are reviewed within 30 days.

6. Children

Students use the App under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. A student ID is issued only after a contract is signed at the center. The App does not collect data from children for advertising or marketing.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page with its date.
